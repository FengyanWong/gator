### 7.381 Round 4 offline results: LOADBench does not help the Round 3 models (2026-10-19, Claude session "Training encoders with Zenodo dataset"; user: "go on, finish rgcn seed 2 then write it up")

**What.** Round 4 (plan section 7b, record 7.363), run offline from 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-19 and written up in the new
plan subsection 7b.7; the plan's status row says so. No online run, no simulation, no change to the workbench or the
optimizer: every script lives outside the repository (`~/gator/worktrees/loadbench-r4/`) and ran on the frozen
runtime `gator-runtime-7356r` (optimizer digest `6ebf5ba5`).

**Result, in plain words.** Three ways of teaching the Round 3 models from LOADBench's 101 SKY130 op-amps left their
error on the user's own held-out designs where more training on the user's runs alone puts it, or made it worse.
The networks learn each LOADBench op-amp they are trained on, but almost nothing that carries over to one they have
