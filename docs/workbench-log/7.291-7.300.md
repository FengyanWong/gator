### 7.291 The Tasks, Runs and RL model windows close once a task, run or saved model is chosen (2026-10-09, session "Frontend workbench layout design"; user: "for the frontend tasks models and runs window, after selecting one target task, model or run, close the pop up window")

**Asked.** Until now each of the three windows said "This window stays open until you close it" (records 7.227, 7.38/7.267,
7.265). Now a choice that went through closes the window it was made in; a choice that did not go through leaves it open,
with the reason where it was said before. What counts as choosing:
- **Tasks window**: the double click on a task. It closes once the page follows the task (`chooseTask` answers
  `{ loaded: true }`); Cancel at the edits question, a task that cannot be read or a newer choice leave it open.
- **Runs window**: the double click (Load run, or Show results where Load run is not offered), and in a run's Details
  **Load run**, **Show results**, **Continue...** and **New run like this**. Show results and Continue... close it at once,
  Load run and New run like this once they went through (`loadRun` / `newRunLike` now answer true or false). **Clear**,
  **Details**, the folders and the links inside the window keep it open.
- **RL model window**: a saved model chosen - a double click on a model, a save point's **Use**, **Use the newer one**, the
  suggested model's **Use** (`useSavePoint` now answers true once the save point is the draft's). A save point that cannot
  be read leaves it open with the reason; New model chosen while a Use is on its way lets that Use go, and the window stays.
  The templates of New model are not a saved model, and the window holds the settings still to set after one is chosen:
  choosing a template keeps it open.

  click and the four Details actions call it; the hints and the button's tooltip say so.

**How the published sizes are drawn in this kit, and four readings of the published text.**
1. A published `w=W*n nf=n m=k` is a transistor of finger width W with n x k copies (`m`), fingers 1: the kit netlists
   finger width and copies, its transistor model bins end at 900 um per device (a published 1.5 mm pass device fits only
   that way), and its form clamps a typed total width at 900 um (7.295 item 13). Same DC; the kit's callbacks then write
   areas and perimeters of an unshared diffusion, so somewhat more junction capacitance than the published fingers.
2. `m=N2+N1` (the amplifier's two PMOS current sources) is two transistors in parallel, one per variable; `m=2*N1` and
   `m=2*N8` are followers with ratio 2. Hence 37 and 63 drawn transistors.
3. LDO: the bias transistor M21 is diode-connected (its gate net VB2 = its drain net4), as the paper's Fig. 6(c) draws it;
   the published text names the two nets apart, which would leave its 2 uA source a dead branch. The two 2 uA sources sit in
   the bench.
4. Amplifier: the bias transistor M27 is drawn as SEVEN in series. The published text prints it once; the published full LDO,
   which flattens the same cell, prints `M27<6:0>` in series, and the paper counts it once in both. With seven, this kit
   reproduces the repository's recorded power of its 17 designs within 2 % and their common-mode phase margin within 2 deg;
   with one, the gain is 20 dB lower and the power 8 to 13 % off. The common-mode loop is cut where the full LDO has its
   probe (pins cmo / cmi). In the full LDO the two published probes are shorts.
